Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 22, 2026

Welcome to JithMa Private Limited. Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy applies to our mobile applications, including Quiz App and Travelog.

2. How Your Data is Used

The data collected is used solely for:

We implement professional-grade encryption to protect your data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third-party marketing firms.

Our apps may use Google Analytics for Firebase to monitor app stability and performance. These services handle data according to their own privacy standards.

You can manage your data directly within the app settings. You have the right to request a full deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

We use the Google Advertising ID in QuizApp Name to provide a better user experience and to support our free services.
